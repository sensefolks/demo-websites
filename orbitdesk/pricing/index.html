A few things you might wonder.

Can I try the workspace without choosing a plan?

Absolutely. The demo workspace is open to everyone and already filled with a sample project. You can complete tasks, switch views, and explore without creating an account.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

That’s the idea. Switch the billing period above to compare the fictional plans. Pro is $15 per person monthly or $12 per person per month when billed yearly. Business is $25 monthly or $20 with yearly billing.

What happens when a team needs to take a break?

A little flexibility goes a long way. Manage your subscription on the account page. Sharing feedback is always optional, and every action in this demo is simulated.

Will I be charged for anything here?

No. OrbitDesk is a fictional Sensefolks demo. There are no real subscriptions, payments, or account registrations. Prices give the example a realistic context.
