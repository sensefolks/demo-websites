Five small guides to a smoother workday.

How do I find my way around a workspace?

Think of your workspace as your team’s shared home. Projects give every idea a place to live, and tasks turn those ideas into small, doable steps.

  1. Open the Website launch project to see the team’s board.
  2. Browse To do, In progress, and Done to get your bearings.
  3. Switch to List for a different view of the same work.

There’s no setup to finish here. We’ve left a fictional project ready for you to explore.

How do I mark a task as complete?

A small win deserves a small celebration. Each unfinished task has a ✓ Done button in its bottom corner.

  1. Find a task on the project board.
  2. Select Done. The card moves into the Done column.
  3. Watch the project’s progress update as your team moves forward.

Your first completed task also offers a chance to help shape our roadmap. You can close that invitation and keep working.

Can I create a task or use a project template?

Yes. Select New task above your board, give the task a name, and choose a category. It will appear in To do, ready for its first step.

For a little head start, open Templates in the workspace sidebar. The launch checklist adds three practical tasks to your current project. You can also open the template directly.

This demo keeps your changes in the current browser session. Use Start again in the footer to return to the original project.

Where can I compare or change my plan?

The pricing page has a side-by-side look at Free, Pro, and Business. Toggle monthly and yearly billing to find the right fit.

You’ll also find a Help price Pro button there. It opens a pricing feedback example alongside the plan’s features, while the value is still fresh in your mind.

Visit Your account for a fictional subscription summary. No plan selection creates a real subscription or charge.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Your account and find Need to take a pause? beneath your billing information.

  1. Select Cancel subscription.
  2. You’ll see an optional feedback space.
  3. Select Confirm cancellation to finish. You never need to answer a survey first.

The cancellation in this demo is simulated. You can restore the fictional plan immediately afterward.
