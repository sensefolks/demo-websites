The details that matter

The pour-over holds a standard cone filter. The mug holds 300ml of your favorite brew. Our medium-roast blend is imagined with comforting notes of chocolate and hazelnut. Designed for one lovely cup at a time.

Your five-minute ritual

Warm your mug, rinse your filter, and add 18g of ground coffee. Pour a little hot water, pause for 30 seconds, then slowly add the rest to reach 300ml. Take a breath. Enjoy.
