A note from the editors

You don’t have to go far to feel far away.

We started Fieldnotes for the days between big adventures. The nearby hill you have never climbed. The café at the end of a footpath. The conversation that runs a little longer than expected.

We believe the outdoors is for ordinary weekends, too. These are stories to help you notice more, carry less, and find your own way out there.
